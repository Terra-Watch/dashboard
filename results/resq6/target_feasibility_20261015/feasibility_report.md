# RESQ6 target-definition and data-availability assessment

Audit timestamp: 2026-10-14 22:22:59 UTC (2026-10-15 in Asia/Kolkata). Scope is limited to the four established development incidents: E2024-131853, E2024-135332, E2023-084925, and E2024-155286. No held-out data were accessed. No network requests, acquisition, simulations, rescoring, or label/model changes were made.

## Executive finding

The local observations best support investigating **cumulative detected active-fire presence**, not fire onset or true burned-area spread. Even that task is only conditionally evaluable: the event-linked VIIRS detections provide positives, but no clear-sky/valid-observation mask exists in the local inputs, so non-detections cannot be called valid negatives. In addition, seven of the eight official 24 h/48 h comparisons use a selected observation 10.28–35.23 hours before its forecast horizon; the remaining gap is 0.63 hours, with no approved age tolerance. All four cases lack an eligible event-linked n/h pass by 6 h and 12 h.

Therefore, **none of the four nominal horizons currently supports an unqualified, complete-cell predictive evaluation from these data**. 6 h and 12 h are not feasible with current observations. 24 h and 48 h are conditionally feasible after an owner-approved target-age policy and acquisition/derivation of valid-observation coverage. Historical official scores remain the frozen benchmark against the existing detection-mask semantics; this report does not reinterpret or replace them.

### Coverage and pre-origin evidence

No local source has a verified per-cell clear-sky, cloud, valid-observation, or satellite-view opportunity mask for the VIIRS target. FIRMS `source_file`, satellite, day/night, and scan/track metadata remain, but do not establish which other cells were observable and clear. Absence of a row is consequently **unknown**, not a defensible negative. For MCD64A1, the local derived products contain burned pixels and uncertainty, not a validated complete observation mask for the target protocol.

The raw CSV has 11 pre-t0 detections for E2023-084925, of which three are inside the model domain; these were excluded from the selected connected component. They demonstrate that pre-origin detections exist in the broader fetched CSV, not that the selected incident cell set was previously unburned or that those points belong to this incident. No pre-t0 raw rows appear in the other three CSVs. None of these facts proves clear-sky/no-fire conditions.

## Three target semantics

| Task | Prediction unit and target | Valid positives / negatives / unknowns | Current-data judgement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Fire onset** | Cell is fire-free at forecast origin, then experiences its first qualifying fire detection during `(t0, t0+h]`. Origin must be an explicit operational origin; current t0 is a proxy 12 h before first selected detection. | Positive requires a newly affected cell with a qualifying detection in the interval. Negative requires the cell to have been known unburned at origin and observed with adequate valid coverage throughout the relevant opportunity window. Cloudy, unobserved, ambiguous, or pre-existing cells are unknown/excluded. | Not supported now: no verified baseline no-fire state or coverage masks; t0 is not known ignition. At 6/12 h no associated qualifying post-origin pass exists. 24/48 h are only conditionally feasible with pre-origin/coverage data and approved event association. |
| **B. Cumulative detected fire presence** | Cell has at least one qualifying active-fire detection in the incident-linked cumulative set by the target observation cutoff. It measures detected presence, not burn status or perimeter. | Positive = qualifying event-linked detection. Negative only if adequate valid observation opportunity through cutoff and no qualifying detection. Otherwise unknown. | Closest fit to extant labels because the current label is exactly a cumulative n/h detection-cell union. However coverage and age policy are missing; current complete-cell AP/Brier labels are not defensible as fire absence. No eligible pass by 6/12 h. 24/48 h conditional. |
| **C. Spatial spread** | Newly affected area / spatial change from a well-defined observed initial footprint at t0 to a later specified time. | Positive = newly burned/affected cells within the interval from independent perimeter or well-observed change data. Negative requires valid coverage and reliable baseline; unchanged/unobserved cells distinguished. | Not supported as true burned-area spread: no incident-linked initial footprint at t0, intermittent VIIRS overpasses, no coverage mask, and MCD64A1 is monthly/coarse/uncertain. A narrower “newly detected VIIRS cells” proxy is conditionally possible at observed passes after coverage and baseline decisions; it must not be called true spread. No post-origin pass by 6/12 h. |

For all tasks, the model prediction grid and target grid need an explicit common CRS, cell size, and mapping. Existing code uses EPSG:32644 and 30 m simulation cells but labels derive from 375 m nominal keys. Use of whole 375 m keys induces an approximation; a defensible future target must state whether it retains that key mapping or uses actual scan/track pixel footprints. Incident membership and target cutoff must be fixed without future detections leaking into origin selection, candidate selection, tuning, or train/test assignment.

The 24 h 131853 case is provisionally nearest in time, but “0.63 h” is not declared acceptable. No tolerance is adopted here. An observation after a horizon cannot be substituted without changing target semantics and risking look-ahead. A target-age rule must consider task, acquisition cadence, expected fire-front motion and uncertainty, and whether the horizon means “state at exactly h” or “state at nearest eligible observation.” The owner should approve the policy before scores are generated; do not fill missing target cells as negative or interpolate labels without a separate validated observation model.

For 24/48 h, minimum additional requirements are: (1) valid observation opportunity/quality masks aligned to each acquisition and cell (or a documented suitable independent source); (2) an approved maximum lag/eligibility rule and treatment of cases with no eligible pass; (3) a verified incident association and origin baseline; and (4) explicit spatial-footprint mapping/resolution. For onset, add sufficient pre-origin observations to establish at-risk cells. For actual spread, add time-resolved mapped perimeter/burned-area observations; current monthly MCD64A1 is insufficient. To score 6/12 h, a relevant validated observation source must supply qualifying observations by those horizons; no such current observation exists.

## Recommended future evaluation protocol (proposal, not approved)

**Recommend task B, cumulative detected active-fire presence, as the primary near-term research target** because it matches the extant positive-label construction directly and avoids claiming that VIIRS points are complete burn perimeters. This recommendation does not make current complete-cell scores valid; obtain coverage and resolve timing first. Keep task C, true spread, as a separate future target requiring independent perimeter data. Do not call task A “onset” until pre-origin state and coverage support that interpretation.

Proposed protocol:

1. Freeze the forecast origin per incident before constructing targets. If retaining current t0, describe it as the protocol origin (first selected detection minus 12 h, floored), not physical ignition. Decide whether incidents with pre-origin detections outside the selected component can be retained, excluded, or re-associated under a predeclared rule.
2. Keep nominal horizons 6/12/24/48 h, but score a horizon only if an eligible observation exists under an owner-approved age rule. Use actual acquisition time as the target timestamp; do not silently use a later pass, shift the prediction frame, or interpolate.
3. Build a per-cell, per-time status: `positive` for incident-linked n/h detection by cutoff; `negative` only if coverage/quality proves a valid observation opportunity and no qualifying detection; otherwise `unknown`. Exclude unknown cells from binary metrics and publish covered/positive/unknown cell counts and coverage maps beside scores.
4. Publish the exact CRS and rasterization rule. Either explicitly retain the existing EPSG:32644 / global 375 m-key mapping onto 30 m centers, or approve a footprint-aware alternative based on source pixel geometry; do not mix resolutions across cases.
5. Prevent leakage: event/origin assignment and features use only information available by forecast origin; future detections are labels only. Split at incident level, freeze train/validation/test membership and label rules before tuning; test incidents remain untouched until the development gate and target policy are approved.
6. Report per incident and horizon, not pooled as independent observations. AP and Brier may be reported on covered cells with prevalence and valid-cell denominator; interpret AP as ranking of detected presence and Brier as probability error for that observed target, not burned-area skill. For a future task C, add explicit spatial-change/perimeter metrics (e.g. area overlap and boundary distance) under a separately frozen protocol.
7. Reproduction bundle must include immutable raw source files and checksums, product/version and acquisition metadata, coverage/quality masks, event-association and origin records, target arrays/status masks, exact selected timestamps and age gaps, code revision/source hashes, model/member artifacts, scoring code and environment. Preserve official historical scores and report any new protocol as a separate benchmark.
