Assumptions (4)
  • Fuel: derived from ESA WorldCover land cover, mapped to Spanish fuel models (TL2 forest, GR1 grass). Uncalibrated — no canopy, load or moisture data. India-specific fuel mapping is blocked (not yet available).
  • Weather: ERA5-Land (primary). Temperature and humidity are fixed at 27°C / 40% by the simulator regardless of actual conditions.
  • Terrain: Copernicus GLO-30 elevation. Slope has almost no effect on this result (RESQ-5 measured less than 1% effect at a 20° slope).
  • Ignition: point and time are estimates from the first satellite detection, not a confirmed exact ignition.
