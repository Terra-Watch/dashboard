Honest conclusions (docs/RESQ5_RESULTS.md §6)
  1. Skill beyond location is not shown. The proximity baseline has the higher AUC in 4 of 4 incidents and the higher AP in 4 of 4.
  2. The primary-wind ensemble can be empty, and one calm hour decides it.
  3. Footprint size is over-predicted where the fire does not die.
  4. With 3-day windows, 0-1 hours per source fall below the no-spread threshold.
  5. Edge clipping: none, 0 of 90 members touch the outer ring in any incident.
  6. In three of four incidents the simulated area overtakes the observed footprint from 48-72h.
  7. This does not validate the model, the fuel mapping, the ROS curve, or the 12h ignition lead.
